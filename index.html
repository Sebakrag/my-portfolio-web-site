About Me

I’m a computer engineering student who enjoys building things and understanding how they work under the hood. I’m particularly interested in backend development, system communication, and the role infrastructure plays in supporting modern applications.

I like working close to the fundamentals: how services interact, how data flows through a system, and how design decisions affect reliability and security. Along the way, I’ve been developing projects that help me better understand real-world constraints such as scalability, maintainability, and fault tolerance.

Recently, I’ve been focusing more on networking concepts and cybersecurity fundamentals, aiming to understand how secure systems are designed and operated. My goal is to grow into roles where engineering quality, reliability, and security are essential.
